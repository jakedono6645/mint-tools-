Recurring Service

Standard Residential Cleaning

Our recurring service keeps your home consistently clean visit after visit. Every clean follows the same thorough standard — no shortcuts.

Throughout the Home
  • Dust all accessible surfaces — flat surfaces, shelves, décor
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Sweep and mop hard floors
  • Empty trash (client-provided liners)
  • Clean mirrors and interior glass
Kitchen
  • Wipe exterior of all appliances (stove, fridge, dishwasher, microwave)
  • Clean and sanitize countertops and backsplash
  • Clean sink and faucet
  • Spot clean cabinet exteriors
Bathrooms
  • Clean and disinfect toilets, tubs, and showers
  • Clean vanities, sinks, and faucets
  • Clean mirrors
  • Sweep and mop floors
Bedrooms & Living Areas
  • Dust surfaces and furniture
  • Make beds (if linens are left out)
  • Vacuum and mop floors
  • Empty trash

48-Hour Re-Clean Guarantee

Any concerns must be reported within 48 hours with photos. We'll return one time to re-clean the specific area at no charge. We don't offer refunds — we make it right.

Deep Clean

Deep Clean Service

Everything in our standard clean, plus the high-effort tasks that build up over time. Ideal for first-time service, seasonal resets, or homes that need a thorough top-to-bottom refresh.

Kitchen — Deep Clean Additions
  • Interior of microwave
  • Interior of oven
  • Interior of refrigerator (available as add-on or quoted separately)
  • Full degrease of cabinet exteriors — not just spot clean
  • Backsplash scrubbed including grout lines
  • Range hood and vent cleaned
  • Small appliance exteriors detailed (toaster, coffee maker, etc.)
  • Around stove drip pans and under burners where accessible
Bathrooms — Deep Clean Additions
  • Tile and grout scrubbed in showers and tubs
  • Soap scum and hard water buildup treated
  • Detailed cleaning of toilet base, behind toilet, and hinges
  • Vanity exterior detailed (interior on request)
  • Exhaust fan covers dusted and wiped
Whole Home — Deep Clean Additions
  • Baseboards hand-wiped along entire perimeter
  • Door frames, doors, and door handles wiped
  • Light switches and outlet covers wiped
  • Interior window sills and tracks cleaned
  • Interior glass on ground-level windows
  • Blinds dusted slat by slat where reasonable
  • Ceiling fans wiped (blades and housing)
  • Vents and air returns dusted and wiped
  • Décor, picture frames, and shelves — items moved, not dusted around
  • Behind and under furniture that can be reasonably moved
Bedrooms & Living Areas — Deep Clean Additions
  • Headboards and bed frames dusted and wiped
  • Lamp bases and shades dusted
  • Closet floors vacuumed where accessible
Not Included in Deep Clean
  • Carpet shampooing
  • Inside of cabinets and drawers
  • Interior windows above ground level
  • Wall washing
  • Floor stripping or waxing
  • Anything requiring a ladder beyond a 2-step
  • Move-in / move-out cleans (separate scope and pricing)
  • Post-construction cleaning (separate scope and pricing)

48-Hour Re-Clean Guarantee

Report any concerns within 48 hours with photos. We'll return one time to address the specific area at no charge. Cancellations within 24 hours of service may be subject to a cancellation fee.

Post-Construction

Post-Construction Final Clean

Detail-level finish work performed after construction is cleared by trades. Our team works ceiling-to-floor in every room so no dust settles back onto cleaned surfaces — delivered to a move-in-ready standard.

Whole-Home Detail (Every Room)
  • Ceilings dusted — construction dust, cobwebs, and debris
  • Ceiling fans wiped (blades, motor housing, light kits)
  • All light fixtures wiped including bulbs and decorative glass
  • Recessed light trims wiped clean
  • HVAC vents and return covers vacuumed and wiped
  • Smoke and CO detectors dusted
  • Crown molding wiped along entire run
  • Walls spot-cleaned for dust, fingerprints, and smudges
  • Door frames, door faces (both sides), and door tops wiped
  • Doorknobs, hinges, and hardware wiped
  • Window casings, sills, and tracks vacuumed and wiped
  • Baseboards wiped along entire perimeter
  • Chair rails, wainscoting, and decorative millwork detailed
  • Switch plates, outlet covers, and thermostats wiped
  • Interior windows cleaned (glass, frames, tracks, and sills)
  • All mirrors cleaned streak-free
  • Hard floors vacuumed then mopped with surface-appropriate solution
  • Tile and grout lines detailed — haze and residue removed
  • Carpets vacuumed including edges and corners
  • Stair treads, risers, banisters, spindles, and handrails detailed
Kitchen — Full Detail
  • Cabinet exteriors wiped (faces, sides, tops, and bottoms)
  • Cabinet interiors wiped — every shelf, every drawer, including drawer tracks
  • Cabinet hardware wiped (knobs, pulls, hinges)
  • Toe kicks and under-cabinet trim detailed
  • Pantry shelving wiped inside and out, top to bottom
  • Countertops cleaned with surface-appropriate products
  • Backsplash detailed including grout lines
  • Refrigerator: exterior, interior shelves, drawers, gaskets, and dispenser
  • Oven and range: exterior, control panel, knobs, interior, racks, and glass
  • Range hood: exterior, filter, and interior surfaces
  • Microwave: exterior, interior, turntable, and vent grille
  • Dishwasher: exterior face, control panel, gasket, and interior
  • Sink basin scrubbed; faucet and fixtures wiped clean
Bathrooms — Full Detail (Each)
  • Vanity cabinet exteriors and interiors wiped
  • Countertops cleaned, sink basin scrubbed
  • Mirror cleaned streak-free; medicine cabinet interior wiped
  • Shower glass cleaned both sides
  • Shower walls scrubbed — tile and grout detailed
  • Showerhead, handles, and fixtures wiped and descaled
  • Tub interior, exterior, and surround scrubbed
  • Soap niches, shelves, and shower benches wiped
  • Toilet bowl scrubbed inside and out including under rim and base
  • Tank, lid, and seat cleaned and disinfected
  • Tile floors mopped and grout detailed
  • Exhaust fan covers vacuumed and wiped
Closets, Laundry & Utility
  • All closet shelving wiped top to bottom including upper shelves
  • Closet rods wiped, floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Washer and dryer exteriors, tops, and sides wiped
  • Utility sink scrubbed (if present)
  • Laundry room floors mopped and baseboards detailed
  • Utility and mechanical space floors swept and mopped
Not Included — Available on Request
  • Exterior windows (outside glass)
  • Power washing of exterior surfaces or driveways
  • Garage cleaning or detailing
  • Carpet steam cleaning or extraction (vacuum only included)
  • Hardwood refinishing, buffing, or specialty floor sealing
  • Removal of construction debris or trash hauling
  • Window treatments beyond a light dust
